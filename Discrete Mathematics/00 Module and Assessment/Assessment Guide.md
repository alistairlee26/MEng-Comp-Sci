## How to practise

First use the weekly sheets to learn the methods. After each topic, attempt a new problem without a worked example beside you. Explain why the hypotheses of a theorem apply, justify nontrivial proof steps and practise counterexamples as well as calculations.

Use the [past-exam index](../Past%20Papers/Exam%20Question%20Index.md) for mixed practice once the prerequisites are secure. Solutions are available for the examples in Lists 1, 2, 3 and 5. Later-sheet solutions are not yet visible. Check Moodle after Tuesday PBL sessions, following the lecturer's 11 November announcement.

Before revision season, collect the mock and any complete papers, record the actual exam conditions and update the syllabus using new lecturer guidance. Broader Moodle reading items that are not fully developed in the slides are explicitly retained in the syllabus's scope notes.
