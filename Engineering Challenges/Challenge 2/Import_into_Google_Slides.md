**Importing your formatted connectivity slide into the shared Google Slides deck**

Use `Connectivity_Proposal_Slides_Matched.pptx`. This version matches the supplied PDF export's Arial font, regular black 25.2-point titles, title position, white background, grey diagram styling and 10 × 5.625-inch widescreen dimensions. The original deck varies between individual subsystem slides; this version follows the common styling and the existing empty connectivity slide.

1. Open the shared Google Slides presentation.
2. Select slide 6 in the left-hand slide list, immediately before the empty connectivity slide.
3. Select **File → Import slides**.
4. Choose **Upload**, then select `Connectivity_Proposal_Slides_Matched.pptx` from your computer. If the picker only shows Drive files, upload the PowerPoint to Drive first and select it there.
5. Select the slide you want: **slide 1 = MQTT**, **slide 2 = direct HTTP**, **slide 3 = optional backup**. Normally import just one of slides 1 and 2.
6. Leave **Keep original theme** checked. The source slide has already been styled to match your team deck, so this preserves the formatting prepared here.
7. Click **Import slides**.
8. Place the imported slide at position 7 if needed, then delete the old empty connectivity slide. The Data Analysis slide should follow your connectivity slide.
9. Review the imported slide and its speaker notes once in presentation mode. The text, shapes and lines should remain editable.

Google documents File → Import slides, uploading a presentation, selecting individual slides, and the Keep original theme option in its [presentation guide](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/9282978?hl=en).

Import the PowerPoint for editable content. The PDF and PNG versions are previews; inserting a PNG would make the whole slide a single image.

Speaker scripts are embedded in the PowerPoint and also saved in `Connectivity_Presentation_Notes.md`. The direct-HTTP slide remains an alternative proposal subject to confirming local-only access and lab access-point use with staff.
