--- SLIDE 6 ---
Technical Specification
Your 
Challenge
 is to design and develop a small-scale bioreactor system that:
Remotely monitors system parameters.
Provides the user with real-time system information.
Allows the user to control and adjust system parameters.
Logs the system parameters for process quality control.

--- SLIDE 7 ---
Objectives
During this challenge, you will:
Develop an overall system concept.
Break this down into subsystems.
Design, implement, test and refine 
each subsystem.
Integrate the subsystems to demonstrate complete system operation.
