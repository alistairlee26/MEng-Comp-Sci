Context:
●
Tuberculosis(TB) is a bacterial infection usually 
affecting the lungs
●
Most common treatment of TB is through special 
antibiotics, such as rifampicin or isoniazid etc.
●
Only effective if the TB they have contracted is 
not Multi-drug resistant(MDR). 
●
“Only about 2 in 5 people with multidrug-resistant 
TB accessed treatment in 2024.”, according to the 
WHO.
●
Prevention may also be an option
●
A vaccine trains the immune system to attack the 
actual bacterium so isn't affected by the various 
strains
●
Often cheaper as it is a one-time dose compared 
to the months of treatment on antibiotic
