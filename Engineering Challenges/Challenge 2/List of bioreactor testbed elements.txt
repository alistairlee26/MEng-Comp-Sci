The bioreactor testbed consists of three subsystems that may be used independently or combined to create a complete, small-scale, bioreactor system.
Power Supply:

12V, 7AH rechargable lead acid battery with a 12V and 6V output.
Heating:

The heating subsystem consists of:

 A 3Ω, 30W heater element, that can be immersed.
10kΩ NTC thermistor (ND06P00103K). Datasheet available at: http://www.farnell.com/datasheets/86133.pdf
Stirring:

The stirring subsystem consists of:

A 5V DC motor.
A Hall sensor producing 70 pulses per revolution.

The DC motor is set up with a 10-to-1 gear ratio. The Hall sensor and motor have a common +5 V supply pin.

These must be powered from the Arduino +5 V supply, not an external supply.

More information may be found here: https://moodle.ucl.ac.uk/mod/folder/view.php?id=9105547

pH:

The pH monitoring and control system consists of:

A pH probe (PH-100ATC Probe). Datasheet available at: http://www.rapidonline.com/pdf/127752_da_en_01.pdf
PH meter SKU SEN0161: https://wiki.dfrobot.com/PH_meter_SKU__SEN0161_
Two 6V, 1A peristaltic pumps
