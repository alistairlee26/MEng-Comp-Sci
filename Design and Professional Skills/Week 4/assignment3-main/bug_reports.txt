Bugs:

1. Frog does not respawn
Actual: After dying, the frog stays at the fatal position and loses more lives.
Expected: Return to the starting bank after losing one life.
Reproduce: Jump off the left edge, then wait.
Cause: new_life() updates lives without resetting the frog.
Fix: fr_model.py, line 323: call self.frog.reset_position().

2. Safe river landings kill the frog
Actual: Landing on a log or turtle costs a life.
Expected: The frog survives and moves with the platform.
Reproduce: Cross the road and jump onto a turtle.
Cause: A typo assigns the platform to on_long, leaving on_log as None.
Fix: fr_model.py, line 378: change the assignment to on_log = log.

3. Homes do not match the visible openings
Actual: The leftmost home rejects frogs, making the level impossible to finish.
Expected: All five visible homes accept frogs.
Reproduce: After fixing river landings, enter the leftmost opening.
Cause: Home creation advances x too early and creates six entries instead of five.
Fix: fr_model.py, lines 269-272: store five centres before advancing x
line 298: reset exactly five homes.

4. Restart leaves the game frozen
Actual: Pressing r after Game Over resets the board but movement stays disabled.
Expected: Restart begins a playable game.
Reproduce: Lose all lives, press r, then press an arrow key.
Cause: Resetting never restores game_running after Game Over sets it to False.
Fix: fr_model.py, line 355: set self.game_running = True in reset_level().

5. Time running out does not end the game
Actual: Play continues beyond the 120-second limit.
Expected: Game Over unless all five homes were filled in time.
Reproduce: Start a game and wait two minutes without filling the homes.
Cause: The model never checks the deadline.
Fix: fr_model.py, lines 451-453: end unfinished games at expiry
line 328: clear paused so a pending callback cannot resume the game.
