Bug 1: Bombs not dropping

Bombs can only be dropped once the previous bomb collides with an object,
but if the previous bomb misses an object, you will never be able to drop a bomb again.

If the bomb misses an object, but falls through the bottom of the window, the next bomb
should be allowed to drop.

to reproduce, drop a bomb in between buildings such that it doesnt collide with any of the buildings.

Fixed on line 244 of bomber_oo.py

Bug 2: Plane wraps around too far and misses first building

When the plane reaches the left border of the screen it wraps around to the right hand side but appears
too far left of the screen and consequently misses the rightmost building, meaning that the first building
can never be bombed.

Fixed and reported on line 173 of bomber_oo.py

Bug 3: Hidden building on the right hand side of the screen

A building is created outside of the screen on the right hand side due to an indexing error in the function create_buildings

1200 is used instead of CANVAS_WIDTH for the math to calculate how many buildings fits in the screen

Bug fixed on line 228 of bomber_oo.py

Bug 4: Buildings going below ground level

Bombing a building that is close to being levelled causes the height to become negative and go below ground level. 

Bug fixed on line 80 of bomber_oo.py

Bug 5: Plane not being able to land on completely levelled terrain

This bug happens when all the buildings are completely levelled but the plane still crashes and shows game over.
This is because of the collision logic with the levelled buildings still being there when buildings have height of 0
